About

PhD researcher at CENIMAT|i3N · NOVA School of Science and Technology, Lisbon. My work focuses on the surface characterization of nanostructured materials using quantitative X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS).

With a multidisciplinary background in membrane engineering and nanotechnology, I explore innovative materials for energy and sustainability. I work at the intersection of chemistry, physics, and materials science.
